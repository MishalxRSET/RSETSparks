Delete your account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open RSETSparks and tap your avatar (top right) to open Profile.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm with Delete permanently. If asked, sign out, sign back in and try again.

Without the app

Email u2504040@rajagiri.edu.in from your @rajagiri.edu.in address with the subject "Delete my RSETSparks account". We will delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your sign-in account, profile, photo, likes/passes, blocks, matches and the messages you sent are permanently deleted.

What is kept

Safety reports you filed or that were filed about you are kept for up to 12 months for campus safety, then deleted. Messages your matches sent to you are removed from your view and are no longer linked to your account.
