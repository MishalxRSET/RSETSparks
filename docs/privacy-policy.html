Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

RSETSparks ("we", "the app") is an independent, student-run app for verified students of Rajagiri institutions who are 18 or older. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. RSETSparks is not an official service of Rajagiri School of Engineering & Technology or any Rajagiri institution.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or device advertising ID. We do not show ads, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

How it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage), which encrypts it in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access is restricted by server-side security rules: only verified Rajagiri accounts can read profiles, and only matched students can read their chats. Chats are not end-to-end encrypted.

Moderation

When you report someone, the report includes the last messages of your conversation so moderators can review it. Moderators can suspend accounts. Every moderator action, including any review of a conversation, is recorded in an audit log.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete Account) or by following these steps. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, photo, likes/passes, blocks, matches and the messages you sent. Reports you filed, and reports filed about you, are kept for up to 12 months for campus safety and then deleted.

Your choices

  • Hide your profile from the Sparks deck at any time (Profile → visibility switch).
  • Unmatch or block anyone from the chat menu.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, by emailing support.

Age requirement

RSETSparks is only for people aged 18 or over. We delete accounts we learn belong to anyone younger.

Changes

We will update this page and the date above if this policy changes, and notify you in the app for significant changes.
